Ancient Temples of Japan

Trip Overview

Discover the spiritual heart of Japan on this 12-day journey through Kyoto and Nara. You'll explore serene temples, wander through bamboo forests, and experience the timeless beauty of traditional Japanese culture.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1-3: Kyoto's Eastern Temples

Arrive in Kyoto and explore the historic Higashiyama district, visiting Kiyomizu-dera Temple and the geisha district of Gion.

Day 4-6: Zen Gardens & Golden Pavilions

Visit the iconic Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), the tranquil Ryoan-ji zen garden, and the stunning Arashiyama Bamboo Grove.

Day 7-9: Nara, The First Capital

Take a day trip to Nara, Japan's first permanent capital. Visit Todai-ji Temple, home to a giant bronze Buddha, and meet the friendly wild deer in Nara Park.
