Patagonia Adventure

Trip Overview

Embark on a 10-day adventure through the rugged, untamed landscapes of Patagonia. From hiking glaciers to witnessing the iconic peaks of Fitz Roy, this trip is for the true adventurer at heart.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1-3: El Chaltén & Fitz Roy

Arrive in El Chaltén, the hiking capital of Argentina. Embark on world-class treks to Laguna de los Tres for breathtaking views of Mount Fitz Roy.

Day 4-6: Perito Moreno Glacier

Travel to El Calafate and witness the awe-inspiring Perito Moreno Glacier. Take a boat tour to see the glacier calving up close.

Day 7-9: Torres del Paine National Park

Cross into Chile to explore the stunning Torres del Paine National Park, famous for its granite pillars, turquoise lakes, and abundant wildlife.
