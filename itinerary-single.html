Roman Culinary Journey

Trip Overview

Embark on an 8-day gastronomic adventure through Rome. From hands-on cooking classes to tours of historic food markets, you'll taste the very best of the Eternal City. This journey is a feast for the senses, blending iconic sights with authentic culinary traditions.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Aperitivo

Arrive in Rome, check into your boutique hotel, and join us for a classic Italian aperitivo to kick off your journey.

Day 2: Ancient Rome & Trastevere Tasting

Explore the Colosseum and Roman Forum, followed by a guided food tour through the charming streets of Trastevere.

Day 3: Vatican City & Pasta Making

Visit the Vatican Museums and St. Peter's Basilica. In the evening, learn to make fresh pasta from a local chef.
