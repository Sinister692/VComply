Bali Wellness Retreat

Trip Overview

Recharge your body and mind on this 7-day wellness retreat in the spiritual heart of Bali. Through daily yoga, meditation, and healthy organic meals, you'll find balance and tranquility amidst lush rice paddies and serene temples.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1-2: Arrival in Ubud & Settling In

Arrive in Ubud, the cultural center of Bali. Check into your wellness resort and begin to unwind with a gentle evening yoga session.

Day 3-4: Yoga, Meditation & Healthy Eating

Embrace a daily routine of morning yoga, guided meditation sessions, and workshops on mindful eating with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients.

Day 5-6: Balinese Culture & Spa Treatments

Experience a traditional water temple purification ceremony. Indulge in rejuvenating Balinese spa treatments to soothe your body and soul.
